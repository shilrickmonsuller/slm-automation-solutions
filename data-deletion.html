How to Request Data Deletion

If you have interacted with an automation or messaging service operated by SLM Automation Solutions, you may request deletion of personal information that we control.

  1. Send an email to monsullershilrick@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request.
  3. Describe the service or WhatsApp conversation associated with your request.
  4. Provide only the information reasonably necessary to help us locate your records and verify your request.

What Happens Next?

We will review your request, take appropriate steps to verify your identity, and determine which information we control. We will delete eligible information or explain any applicable retention requirements.

Information Managed by Clients

If your information is controlled by a business client using our automation services, we may direct your request to that business or assist it in processing the request.

Third-Party Platforms

Information held independently by WhatsApp, Meta, or other third-party providers may be subject to their own privacy and deletion procedures.
